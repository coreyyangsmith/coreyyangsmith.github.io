Presented an invited technical deep-dive at [YYC DataCon 2026](https://www.yycdata.ca/datacon/datacon-2026) (Stage 3, Room 212; 3:25–4:00 pm) at the BMO Centre in Calgary, AB, Canada on September 11, 2026.

The talk examined why and when multi-agent systems are useful, compared trade-offs across orchestration, decomposition, specialization, and coordination patterns, and explored how retrieval can be integrated into an agent's reasoning process. Drawing on lessons from developing Oliver, an AI system for public procurement, it closed with practical considerations for production systems, including reliability and fault handling, LLM evaluations, telemetry, and balancing latency, cost, accuracy, and user trust.
